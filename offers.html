Before you click

Help your order track correctly

  • Empty your store cart first, then add items after clicking through from Dealena.
  • Finish the purchase in the same browser session, without visiting other deal or coupon sites.
  • Allow cookies for the store, and pause ad blockers while you shop.
  • Use only the coupon codes listed with the offer.
  • Keep your order confirmation email until the cashback is paid.

Full conditions are in the Cashback Terms.
