The life of a cashback order

Four statuses, always visible

  1. Tracked Step 1

    You clicked a Dealena offer and the store reported your order to us.

  2. Pending Step 2

    The store waits out its return and cancellation window before approving.

  3. Confirmed Withdrawable

    The store approved the order. The cashback can now be withdrawn.

  4. Paid To you

    Sent to your UPI ID or Indian bank account.

Where cashback comes from

Stores pay for referrals. We pass part of it on.

Online stores run affiliate programmes that pay a commission when someone they didn't reach on their own makes a purchase. When that purchase comes through a Dealena link, the commission comes to us, and we share a part of it with you.

You pay the store's normal price. Cashback is calculated on the amount the store pays commission on, and the store decides whether an order qualifies.

Good to know before you shop

  • Cashback is usually calculated on the order value after discounts, without taxes, shipping or convenience fees.
  • The rate shown when you click is the rate that applies to your order.
  • Returned, cancelled or exchanged orders don't earn cashback.
  • Using a coupon code that isn't listed with the offer can stop the order from tracking.
