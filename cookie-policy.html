Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on dealena.in and when you click through to a store. Read it with our Privacy Policy.

Cookies we use

TypePurposeSet by
EssentialKeep you signed in, protect forms against misuse and remember security settings. The website can't work properly without them.Dealena
Affiliate trackingRecord that you clicked a Dealena offer, so the store can attribute your order and pay commission. Without them, cashback can't be tracked.Stores and affiliate networks, when you click an offer

We do not use advertising cookies on dealena.in. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details to deliver them.

Affiliate tracking cookies

When you click through from Dealena, the store or its affiliate network sets a cookie on its own website. These cookies are controlled by the store or network under their own privacy policies. They usually last from a single session to 30 days, depending on the store. Blocking them, clearing them before you finish your order, or using private browsing modes that block them can stop your cashback from tracking.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of Dealena may not work. If you block third-party or tracking cookies, orders may not track and cashback may not be paid.

Changes

If we start using other types of cookies, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Questions about cookies: support@dealena.in.
