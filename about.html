About Dealena

Dealena is an online cashback platform for shoppers in India, based in Moirang, Manipur.

What we do

We connect shoppers with store cashback

Dealena lists offers from online stores that run affiliate programmes. When you shop through a Dealena link, the store can see the sale came from us. Once the store confirms the order and pays its commission, we share part of it with you as cashback.

We don't sell products, handle deliveries or take payment for your purchases. Your order, its price, delivery and returns stay between you and the store.

How we earn

Commission, shared openly

Dealena earns from the commission stores pay for referred sales. The cashback shown on each offer is the part we pass on to you. Keeping this simple means we have no reason to push one store over another: you choose where to shop.

Store names and logos shown on Dealena belong to their owners and are used only to identify where an offer applies.

How we work

Our principles

Show the real status

Cashback is pending until a store confirms it. We say so plainly and never present pending amounts as money you already have.

Write rules down first

Eligibility, validation and payout conditions are published in our Cashback Terms before you shop.

Collect only what we need

We ask for the details needed to track orders and pay you, and handle them as described in our Privacy Policy.

Reachable by email

Questions about orders, payouts or your account go to one address: support@dealena.in.

Where we are

Based in Manipur, open to shoppers across India

Dealena operates from Moirang in Bishnupur District, Manipur. Our service is available to members living anywhere in India.
